Privacy Policy — Osaka Archives

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This policy describes the owner's personal Osaka Archives integration with Google Drive using rclone. It is not a public multi-user application.

Google Drive access

The integration uses Google OAuth authorization to list, upload, download, verify, and manage archive files as directed by the owner. The configured Google Drive scope can permit access across the authorized account; the selected archive folder is a configuration boundary, not a Google-enforced folder-only permission.

Use and storage

Archive files and related metadata are used for storage, restoration, verification, and owner-directed research. Files are stored in the owner's Google Drive and may be read or temporarily stored on the owner's server during these operations. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in private configuration files on the owner's server.

Sharing

This integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google provides the cloud storage and authorization services. The owner may separately direct applications or assistants they operate to analyze archive contents; those tools are governed by their own terms and the owner's instructions.

Retention and control

The owner controls archive retention and deletion through their Google Drive and server. Disconnecting OAuth access does not itself delete archived files. The owner can revoke the integration's authorization in their Google Account's third-party connections settings and remove local credentials from the server.

Website and contact

These informational pages do not contain archive files, sign-in forms, or application credentials. They include no application analytics scripts. The website hosting provider may process standard request logs under its own privacy policy. For questions about this personal integration, use the support email displayed on its Google authorization screen.
